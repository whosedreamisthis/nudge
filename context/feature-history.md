- **Dates & Streaks Logic:** Added two pure modules with no database or framework imports. `lib/dates.ts` represents a user-local calendar day as a `DateKey` string (`YYYY-MM-DD`, matching the `@db.Date` columns) so a day never shifts with the server's timezone: `toDateKey()`/`todayKey()` turn an instant into the local day for an IANA timezone, `addDays()`/`daysBetween()` do day arithmetic in UTC (immune to daylight-saving jumps), `weekStart()`/`weekDays()` use Monday-Sunday weeks, and `fromDbDate()`/`toDbDate()` convert to and from Prisma's UTC-midnight `Date`. `lib/streaks.ts` takes plain `Completion`, `StreakTarget` and `today` values: `isDayComplete()` counts a day only when `amount >= dailyGoal` (goal 1 for simple habits), `completedDays()` sums amounts per day, `targetForWeek()` picks the newest target starting on or before a week (null before the first), `isWeekComplete()`/`completedInWeek()` count completed days Monday to Sunday, and `getStreak()` returns `{ unit, current, best }`. The unit comes from the target in effect this week: a daily target gives a day streak and a weekly target a week streak. Streaks are strict: one missed day resets a day streak, but today stays open until the day ends (an unchecked today does not break a streak reaching yesterday), and an unfinished current week neither extends nor resets a week streak. Past weeks are judged by the target in effect then, weeks before a habit's first target never count, completions dated after today are ignored, and a habit with no targets falls back to a day streak. Decided with the user: weeks start on Monday and streaks are strict with no grace day for now; both are recorded in the open questions of `context/project-overview.md`. 42 new Vitest tests (99 total) cover timezone midnight rollovers (Los Angeles, Auckland), the 1 Nov 2026 clock change, leap days, month and year boundaries, Sunday-to-Monday rollover, multi-step goals, target changes mid-streak, and a habit switching between weekly and daily. Nothing calls these modules yet; Today, Progress and the AI report will. Known follow-up: when a habit changes frequency, the streak uses only the current frequency's unit and does not blend the earlier history.
- **Today Page: Habit List:** `/today` now greets the user ("Hey {first name}", with the long date in their timezone from `formatLongDate()` in `lib/dates.ts`) and lists their active habits. The page calls `await connection()` first (everything on it is per user and per request, and Prisma needs `crypto.randomUUID()`), then `auth.protect()`, then loads the data; `app/(app)/today/loading.tsx` is the Suspense boundary, a skeleton of a header and three rows. `lib/db/today.ts` `getTodayHabits(userId, today)` queries non-archived habits for the user (oldest first) with category, targets and completions, and maps each to a `TodayHabit` (`types/habits.ts`) with a streak from `getStreak()` and today's progress from the new `lib/progress.ts` `getTodayProgress()` (amount today, daily goal, complete, and `week: { done, target }` for weekly habits). Complete rows get a light wash of the theme's completion colour (green in light mode, sky blue in dark) and a filled circle with a tick; multi-step habits show a native `<progress>` bar in the habit's colour with `amount/goal unit` (e.g. `10/20 min`); weekly habits show `2/3 this week`; incomplete rows are plain cards with an empty circle.
